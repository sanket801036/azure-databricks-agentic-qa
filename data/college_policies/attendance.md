Sample policy for the demo college that ships with College ERP. It is written to match
the rules the ERP enforces; it is not any real institution's handbook.

## Minimum attendance for examinations

A student must attend at least 75% of the classes held in a course to be eligible to sit
the Semester End Examination for that course. The requirement applies to each course
separately: a high overall percentage does not make up for a single course that is below
75%.

## How the percentage is calculated

Attendance is the number of classes attended divided by the number of classes held in
that course, from the start of the semester to the date it is read. Classes that were
cancelled are not counted as held. A session excused by approved leave is left out of the
calculation entirely: it is counted neither as attended nor as held.

## Students below the threshold

A student who is below 75% in a course is shown, on their dashboard, how many consecutive
classes they must attend in that course to get back to 75%, assuming they attend every
remaining class. Teachers see the same list for the classes they take. There is no
condonation by the department for a shortfall; the only routes back above the threshold
are attending classes and approved leave.

## Disputing an attendance entry

A student who was marked absent but was present may dispute that entry within 7 days of
the class. The teacher who took the register either corrects it or records why the entry
stands. An accepted dispute is recorded in the audit log in the same way as a teacher's own
correction.
