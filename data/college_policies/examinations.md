Sample policy for the demo college that ships with College ERP. It is written to match
the rules the ERP enforces; it is not any real institution's handbook.

## Continuous Internal Evaluation

Each course is assessed through three internal tests, two events and the Semester End
Examination. The Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE) total is calculated from the
internal tests and events, and is published to students through College ERP once the
teacher releases the marks.

## Questioning a published mark

A student who believes a published mark is wrong may raise a query within 7 days of the
marks being published. The student states what they believe is wrong. The teacher who
taught the course either corrects the mark or explains why it stands, and both are
notified of the outcome.

## Limits on corrections

A corrected mark must stay within the maximum marks for that assessment, the same ceiling
the marks-entry form enforces. A re-evaluation cannot be used to award more than the
assessment allows.

## Eligibility for the Semester End Examination

Eligibility to sit the Semester End Examination for a course also depends on attendance;
see the Attendance Regulations.
