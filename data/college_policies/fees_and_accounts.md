Sample policy for the demo college that ships with College ERP. It is written to match
the rules the ERP enforces; it is not any real institution's handbook.

## Paying fees

Fee instalments and their due dates are shown on the student dashboard in College ERP,
together with the total outstanding. Payments are recorded by the accounts office, and a
receipt can be downloaded as a PDF once a payment is recorded. Reminders are sent before a
due date and again if an instalment becomes overdue.

## Signing in

Students sign in to College ERP with the username issued by the college. After five wrong
passwords on one account within fifteen minutes, sign-in for that account is paused for a
while. Signing in successfully clears the count.

## Forgotten passwords

A student who has forgotten their password can reset it themselves. A six-digit code is
sent to the email address on the account; it expires after ten minutes, can be used only
once, and stops working after five wrong attempts. The reset page responds in the same
way whether or not an account exists for the details entered.

## Getting help

A student who cannot sign in can message the administrator from the sign-in page without
being signed in. Questions about a course that the assistant cannot answer can be sent to
the student's teachers from the assistant.
