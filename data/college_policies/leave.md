Sample policy for the demo college that ships with College ERP. It is written to match
the rules the ERP enforces; it is not any real institution's handbook.

## Kinds of leave

Students may apply for medical leave, official duty leave (for example, representing the
college at an event) or personal leave. A single application may cover up to 30 days.

## Applying for leave

Leave is applied for through College ERP. A supporting document, such as a medical
certificate or an event letter, may be attached and is recommended but not mandatory. Any
teacher who takes the student's class may approve or reject the application, and the
student is notified of the decision.

## Effect of approved leave on attendance

Approved leave excuses the absences already marked within its dates, and any session
marked absent later while the leave still applies. An excused session is removed from the
attendance calculation rather than counted as present. If a student attends a class
during approved leave, that class counts as attended in the normal way.

## Rejected leave

If leave is rejected, the absences within its dates continue to count against the
student's attendance. The student may still dispute an individual attendance entry under
the Attendance Regulations.
